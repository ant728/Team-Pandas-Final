# Specifications tried

Every specification we run goes here, including the ones we drop. Count so far: **4 return/measure variants** on the 6-bank proof of concept (Oct 29), plus **2 measurement choices and 4 regressions** on the full sample (Nov 10). Each regression was run once, as listed; none was re-run with different controls or samples beyond what's here.

## Choices fixed before looking at returns
- Call report quarter: Q4 2022 (due Jan 30, 2023, public before the event).
- Day 0: Mar 9, 2023 (SVB's announcement came after the close on Mar 8). Window: trading days 0 to +2.
- Uninsured share denominator: domestic deposits (the uninsured estimate only covers domestic offices).
- Sample: every entity in the NY Fed CRSP–FRB link on Dec 31, 2022, matched to FDIC data, with event returns and positive equity.
- Market: CRSP value-weighted index (`vwretd`).

## Historical screen test design — recorded November 10, 2026; not yet run

This is a retrospective design: the team already knows famous outcomes. It is not an untouched prospective preregistration. Freeze definitions before calculating screen performance; do not tune thresholds to make known cases pass.

- Eligible universe: December 31, 2022 NY Fed-linked listed entities with valid pre-event measures and positive equity. Report exclusions and missing values.
- Screen: average percentile ranks of uninsured domestic deposit share and a consistently available securities exposure measure. Freeze transfer-loss collection and availability rules before scoring; do not apply a special Zions-only adjustment to cross-bank ranks.
- Flag: top 10% of eligible entities; break ties by regulator ID.
- Later distress: regulatory bank failure after March 13 and by December 31, 2023, based on the FDIC list with documented entity matching. Report mergers separately; PacWest's merger is not automatically a failure.
- Report coverage, flag rate, false positives and missed failures without optimizing on outcomes. The dashboard currently uses historical measures, not a validated live prediction.
